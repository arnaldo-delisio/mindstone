# mocs/

Maps of Content — auto-generated index files created by the intelligence pipeline.

## What Goes Here

MOCs are generated automatically when the intelligence pipeline detects a cluster of related notes across your vault. They serve as navigational hubs connecting notes around a theme or topic.

**You do not create MOCs manually.** They are created and updated by the pipeline when it identifies meaningful connections between your notes.

## How They Are Created

When the intelligence pipeline runs, it analyzes embeddings across all vault content. When enough notes cluster around a topic, it generates or updates a MOC file here linking to the related notes.

## Format

MOC files are named by topic (e.g. `machine-learning.md`, `productivity.md`) and contain a curated list of linked notes with brief descriptions of how they relate.
